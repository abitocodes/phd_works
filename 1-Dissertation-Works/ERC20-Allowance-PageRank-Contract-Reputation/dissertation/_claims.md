## Main research question (one sentence)

What do ERC-20 allowance edges add to PageRank as a reputation score for smart contracts on Arbitrum One, compared with the transfer walk and with the raw degrees that both walks smooth?

## Claims C1–C6 (Chapter 4 states them; Chapters 1, 5, 6 refer to them)

- **C1 (cost).** On the same contracts the solver's time follows the edge count. EndorseRank solves the allowance graph (11,210,114 edges) in 10.29 s, AWP the transfer graph (25,001,216 edges) in 22.37 s: a time ratio of 2.17 at an edge ratio of 2.23. C-PR (union, 31,552,359 edges) takes 36.16 s and S-PR 34.42 s, longer than AWP. The speed belongs to the sparser graph, not to the algorithm.
- **C2 (same-window agreement).** Each score agrees most with the proxies of its own edges: EndorseRank 0.585 [0.578, 0.592] on the allowance family, AWP 0.572 [0.564, 0.580] on the transfer family; intended-construct check, partly built in. With USD amounts AWP now follows the summed value of inbound transfers almost as closely as their number (in-value 0.566 [0.558, 0.575], in-degree 0.578 [0.569, 0.587]). EndorseRank leaves few ties (13,625 distinct scores, 0.17% of pairs tied) and the isolated-node rule changes no coefficient. The C-PR mixtures lie between their two layers on every family.
- **C3 (divergence).** EndorseRank and AWP order the contracts differently: τ = 0.174 [0.163, 0.185]; 96.7% of the pairs are ordered by both, and 41.2% of those are discordant.
- **C4 (degree baselines out of window).** AWP ranks later new transfer senders above the transfer in-degree in both windows (W0 +0.043 [0.038, 0.048]; W1 +0.022 [0.017, 0.027]). EndorseRank (uniform restarts) ranks new approval pairs below the in-approve degree in both windows (W0 −0.019 [−0.026, −0.011]; W1 −0.034 [−0.038, −0.029]). With activity restarts it ranks them above the degree in both (W0 +0.059 [0.050, 0.067]; W1 +0.027 [0.021, 0.034]), a post hoc comparison and a hypothesis, not a finding. All degree contrasts were computed after the labels were known. C-PR (λ = 1) minus the in-approve degree in W0: −0.027 [−0.034, −0.019]. Restricted to the 14,094 W0 spenders that had received a transfer: EndorseRank −0.013 [−0.020, −0.006], AWP +0.056 [0.049, 0.064]; 2,891 spenders had no transfer edge.
- **C5 (coupling).** On the revised scores, one walk over both edge types does not beat its layers walked alone. Rule A fails: no worse than the allowance walk on new approval pairs (+0.029 [0.022, 0.036]) but worse than the transfer walk on new senders (−0.034 [−0.040, −0.029]); its same-window part fails too. Rule B is not met: F1 holds (+0.110 [0.098, 0.122]); F2 fails (−0.028 [−0.033, −0.023]); F3 fails narrowly (+0.000 [−0.014, 0.010]: equal point estimates of 0.350, but the lower end lies below −0.01). With AWP as comparator F2 fails clearly (−0.067 [−0.072, −0.061]). Post hoc in W0: C-PR over the transfer walk on new approval pairs +0.092 [0.080, 0.105]; S-PR below the allowance walk on new approval pairs (−0.051 [−0.066, −0.038]) and level with the transfer walk on new senders (−0.001 [−0.004, 0.002]): allowance edges help only when they carry the walk. The registered analysis reached the same verdicts (Table registered-vs-revised).
- **C6 (outcomes built from neither edge type).** On the liquidation-free close rate of GMX V2 contract traders, applying the registered rules to the revised scores: R1 is not met (P in W1 +0.084, 97.5% interval [−0.026, 0.188]; stratified +0.101 [−0.024, 0.231]), although P is positive in W0 (+0.182, 97.5% [0.032, 0.338]); R2 is not met (Q in W1 −0.041, 97.5% [−0.148, 0.064]; W0 −0.014); R3 applies (P on the profitable share −0.196 [−0.364, −0.046], on the non-loss share −0.252 [−0.391, −0.120]). Every recipient avoided liquidation in both windows (37 of 37, 40 of 40) against 72.7% and 75.0% of the others; the samples are small and the transfer count also ranks the label (0.310 [0.184, 0.425] in W1).

## Points the chapters must carry

- Revision: say once, plainly, in each chapter that discusses results (1, 4, 5, 6, abstract) that the amounts were revised after the registered results were known, what changed, and that the registered analysis reached the same verdicts (Appendix~\ref{sec:map-registered}). Do not dramatise it.
- Multi-hop propagation: EndorseRank equals the closed form c(1 + d δ(v)) within 1% for every depth-one spender (12,039 of 14,406 cohort spenders at T_obs) but for only 26.4% of the deeper ones; overall 87.9% at T_obs and 73.7% of the W0 spenders.
- Sybil model on the revised EndorseRank graph (6,219,063 nodes): a closed farm collects 3.17 times its population share at d = 0.85 (2.06 and 8.68 at 0.75 and 0.95); a star of about 22 farm addresses reaches the cohort's 90th percentile, about 2,074 the 99th, about 135,895 the top. A fresh approval takes 95.7% of the median owner's row. An approval of a listed token is as free as any (no balance needed), so the token list does not raise the cost of a farm; it removes events that a token contract can fabricate.
- Robustness: damping moves no family mean of EndorseRank by more than 0.003; the value-slope check (b × 10, b / 10) moves family means by at most 0.005 (0.0054), and the variants agree with the main scores at τ ≥ 0.968. Sample-definition stages move EndorseRank's allowance coefficient from 0.715 at n = 1,000 to 0.585 (not robustness evidence).
- Contract GMX traders are few in 2026; every trader result has wide intervals.
- The cohort is the set of contracts approved by at least three owners on WETH, USDC, USDT0, ARB or WBTC; results apply to such contracts on Arbitrum One in these months.
- Revocations and the drain heuristic rise with every score and most with the in-approve degree (revocations 0.388 [0.376, 0.398] in W0); they track volume and are not offered as evidence.
- Self-destructed contracts: unchanged limitation (EIP-6780, ballet2023eip6780).
