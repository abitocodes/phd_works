[SLIDE 3 / 16]  Problem & Gap
data-title: Problem & Gap
Suggested time: ~75 seconds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[EN]
Let me start with the problem.

Existing on-chain reputation methods—especially Adaptive Weighted PageRank, or AWP—treat token transfers as signals of economic trust.
But transfers are noisy.
Wallets transfer tokens for trading, routing, airdrops, and automated contract activity.
Those actions do not necessarily mean one wallet is endorsing another.

Transfers also create dense graphs.
At chain scale, repeatedly computing transfer-based PageRank becomes expensive in data processing, memory, and convergence time.

The research gap is that ERC-20 approve and permit actions encode something more direct:
they grant another address spending authority over tokens, and that authority remains until it is changed or revoked.

Most graph reputation systems ignore these allowance relationships as first-class edges.
Identity tools such as Passport address personhood and Sybil resistance, but they do not produce a graded financial ranking from endorsement structure.

So the central problem is this:
Can we build a reputation score from approve and permit relationships on Arbitrum One that better captures endorsement semantics, aligns at least as well with observable GMX V2 trading success as transfer-based PageRank, and does so at lower computational cost?

[KO]
먼저 문제부터 말씀드리겠습니다.

기존 온체인 평판 방법—특히 Adaptive Weighted PageRank, 즉 AWP—는 토큰 전송을 경제적 신뢰 신호로 취급합니다.
그러나 전송은 잡음이 큽니다.
지갑은 거래, 라우팅, 에어드롭, 자동 컨트랙트 활동 때문에 토큰을 전송합니다.
그 행위가 반드시 한 지갑이 다른 지갑을 보증(endorse)한다는 뜻은 아닙니다.

전송은 또한 밀집한 그래프를 만듭니다.
체인 규모에서 전송 기반 PageRank를 반복 계산하면 데이터 처리, 메모리, 수렴 시간 비용이 커집니다.

연구 공백은 ERC-20 approve와 permit이 더 직접적인 신호를 담는다는 점입니다.
다른 주소에 토큰 사용 권한을 부여하고, 그 권한은 변경되거나 취소될 때까지 유지됩니다.

대부분의 그래프 평판 시스템은 이런 allowance 관계를 1급 엣지로 다루지 않습니다.
Passport 같은 신원 도구는 인격성·사이빌 저항을 다루지만, 보증 구조로부터 등급형 금융 순위를 만들지는 않습니다.

따라서 핵심 문제는 이렇습니다.
아비트럼 원에서 approve/permit 관계로 평판 점수를 만들어, 보증 의미를 더 잘 반영하고, 전송 기반 PageRank와 비슷하거나 더 나은 GMX V2 거래 성공 정렬을, 더 낮은 계산 비용으로 얻을 수 있는가?

[SLIDE 5 / 16]  Objectives
data-title: Objectives
Suggested time: ~55 seconds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[EN]
The work is organised into five objective groups.

O1 builds the graph pipeline: extract approve and permit events and keep the latest nonzero allowance for each owner-to-spender pair.

O2 implements EndorseRank—Weighted PageRank with damping factor zero point eight five, and no time decay on allowance edges.

O3 reproduces AWP and a transfer-based PageRank baseline on the same GMX-active wallets and the same time window.

O4 evaluates alignment with GMX trading-success proxies using rho and tau, and interprets both redundancy and weak signal.

O5 measures computational efficiency—runtime, memory, and iterations—as we scale toward larger graphs.

The overarching aim is to design, implement, and evaluate EndorseRank as an allowance-based reputation model.

[KO]
목표는 다섯 그룹으로 나뉩니다.

O1은 그래프 파이프라인: approve/permit 이벤트를 추출하고, owner→spender 쌍마다 최신 nonzero allowance를 유지합니다.

O2는 EndorseRank 구현—damping 0.85, allowance 엣지에 시간 감쇠 없음.

O3는 동일 GMX 활성 지갑·동일 기간에서 AWP와 전송 기반 PageRank 베이스라인을 재현합니다.

O4는 ρ·τ로 GMX 거래 성공 프록시와의 정렬을 평가하고, 중복성(ρ≈1)과 약한 신호(ρ&lt;~0.2)를 해석합니다.

O5는 더 큰 그래프로 확장하며 런타임·메모리·반복 횟수 등 계산 효율을 측정합니다.

총괄 목표는 allowance 기반 평판 모델인 EndorseRank를 설계·구현·평가하는 것입니다.

[SLIDE 7 / 16]  Method Overview
data-title: Method Overview
Suggested time: ~70 seconds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[EN]
Methodologically, we keep the wallet set fixed and compare two graphs.

From public Arbitrum logs in BigQuery, we extract GMX PositionDecrease events, qualify wallets with at least three closes, then build an approval graph and a transfer graph on that same cohort.

EndorseRank uses wallets as nodes and owner-to-spender allowances as edges.
The edge weight is the latest nonzero allowance, decimal-adjusted.
We use damping zero point eight five and no time decay, because an approval remains live until it is superseded.

AWP and the transfer baseline use transfer edges weighted by value.
AWP adds logistic time decay on transfer age.

Both methods are then compared on the same GMX-active cohort and window, using trading-success proxies—close-success count, realized gain, and success rate.

Important: these proxies are domain-intuition measures.
They are not loan-repayment ground truth.

[KO]
방법론적으로는 지갑 집합을 고정하고 두 그래프를 비교합니다.

BigQuery의 공개 아비트럼 로그에서 GMX PositionDecrease를 추출하고, 최소 3회 close한 지갑을 선정한 뒤, 동일 cohort에 approval 그래프와 transfer 그래프를 구축합니다.

EndorseRank는 노드를 지갑, 엣지를 owner→spender allowance로 둡니다.
엣지 가중치는 소수점 조정된 최신 nonzero allowance입니다.
damping은 0.85이며, 승인이 대체될 때까지 유효하므로 시간 감쇠는 없습니다.

AWP와 전송 베이스라인은 가치로 가중된 전송 엣지를 사용합니다.
AWP는 전송 연령에 logistic 시간 감쇠를 적용합니다.

두 방법은 동일 GMX 활성 cohort·기간에서 close-success count, realized gain, success rate로 비교합니다.

중요: 이 프록시는 도메인 직관용 지표이며, 대출 상환 ground truth가 아닙니다.

[SLIDE 8 / 16]  Why Arbitrum One (not Ethereum)
data-title: Why Arbitrum
Suggested time: ~90 seconds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[EN]
Let me explain the empirical setting carefully, because the chain choice changed during proposal development.

Earlier framing—and much of the literature—focused on Ethereum mainnet, with validation conceived around lending-style default or liquidation labels.

The approved setting is Arbitrum One, chain ID forty-two thousand one hundred sixty-one, with reputation graphs from public ERC-20 Approval and Transfer logs, and domain validation via GMX V2 perpetual position outcomes.

Why Arbitrum?

First, public BigQuery access: the goog_blockchain_arbitrum_one_us dataset makes Approval and Transfer extraction reproducible.

Second, same-chain GMX PnL: PositionDecrease events carry wallet-attributed realized outcomes on the same EVM chain as the endorsement graph.

Third, L2 fee levels support denser trading activity inside a fixed six-month window.

Fourth, a single-chain design avoids cross-chain wallet aliasing and multi-chain ETL cost.

I also need a clarification that is easy to get wrong.

We did not choose Arbitrum because GMX is currently the global on-chain perpetuals volume leader.
As of twenty twenty-six, Hyperliquid leads perp-DEX volume, open interest, and TVL.
GMX remains a major Arbitrum-native venue with publicly queryable EVM position logs that fit this research design.

In one sentence:
We chose Arbitrum One because that is where GMX V2 provides dense, publicly queryable position outcomes on the same EVM chain as our allowance graph—not because GMX is the global volume leader.

[SLIDE 9 / 16]  Why GMX PositionDecrease
data-title: Why GMX
Suggested time: ~75 seconds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[EN]
Why must we use GMX PositionDecrease data?

In principle, under-collateralized lending defaults or liquidations would be the most direct credit-risk labels.
On Arbitrum One, however, publicly observable unsecured or under-collateralized lending volume is insufficient for a stable evaluation dataset.

Pulling default labels from other chains would break the single-chain design and increase both engineering cost and identity ambiguity.

Therefore, we adopt GMX V2 perpetual long and short PositionDecrease outcomes.
They give dense, wallet-level realized PnL on the same chain as the reputation graphs.

A profitable close—positive base PnL in USD, excluding liquidation—is treated as a functional analog of successfully managing collateralized leveraged exposure.
It is not treated as proof of creditworthiness.

This follows a construct-alignment stance: we judge reputation scores by agreement with observable domain indicators, extending the proxy-validation pattern used by Do and colleagues for transfer in-degree and in-value.

[KO]
왜 GMX PositionDecrease 데이터를 쓸 수밖에 없는가?

원칙적으로 under-collateralized 렌딩 부도·청산이 가장 직접적인 신용위험 라벨입니다.
그러나 아비트럼 원에서는 관측 가능한 무담보·저담보 렌딩 물량이 안정적 평가 집합을 만들기엔 부족합니다.

다른 체인에서 부도 라벨을 가져오면 단일 체인 설계가 깨지고, 엔지니어링 비용과 신원 모호성이 커집니다.

따라서 GMX V2 롱·숏 PositionDecrease 결과를 채택합니다.
평판 그래프와 같은 체인에서 밀집한 지갑 단위 실현 PnL을 제공합니다.

수익성 close—양수 basePnlUsd, 청산 제외—는 담보부 레버리지 노출을 성공적으로 관리한 기능적 유추로 취급합니다.
신용도를 증명하는 것으로 취급하지 않습니다.

이는 construct alignment 입장입니다. Do 등이 전송 in-degree·in-value에 쓴 프록시 검증 패턴을, 관측 가능한 도메인 지표와의 일치로 확장합니다.

[SLIDE 10 / 16]  Supporting Literature
data-title: Supporting Literature
Suggested time: ~70 seconds
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[EN]
Several sources support using perpetual-position outcomes as a usable laboratory.

Chen, Ma, and Nie, twenty twenty-four, analyse decentralised perpetual designs including GMX under an oracle pricing model.
They show that on-chain perpetual activity yields measurable trader behaviour—volume, open interest, liquidation, and leverage.
That supports treating GMX position streams as empirically informative, not arbitrary.

Do and Do, twenty twenty-three, validate PageRank-family scores against domain-intuition proxies.
We extend that pattern from transfer in-degree and in-value to GMX close-success count and realized-gain proxies.

Ghosh and colleagues, twenty twenty-four, emphasise that on-chain credit and risk scoring need wallet-level histories—reinforcing why dense outcome streams matter when lending defaults are sparse.

Cronbach and Meehl provide the measurement-theory basis: construct alignment, not identity with legal credit.

And we state the limitation clearly:
a profitable perpetual trader is not the same construct as a good unsecured borrower.
Alignment may also differ on other venues such as dYdX or Hyperliquid.

[KO]
무기한 포지션 결과를 “쓸 수 있는 실험실”로 보는 데 여러 문헌이 지지합니다.

Chen, Ma, Nie(2024)는 오라클 가격 모델 아래 GMX를 포함한 DEX 퍼프 설계를 분석합니다.
온체인 퍼프 활동이 거래량·미결제약정·청산·레버리지 등 측정 가능한 트레이더 행동을 낳음을 보여, GMX 포지션 스트림이 임의가 아니라 실증적으로 유의미함을 지지합니다.

Do & Do(2023)는 PageRank 계열 점수를 도메인 직관 프록시로 검증합니다.

Sampling parameters—window, minimum closes, and cohort—fix which events enter the study and ensure both methods see the same wallets.
