1 · Brief topic summary

EndorseRank in context

  • Problem: DeFi wallets lack traditional credit signals. Transfer-graph rankings can be computationally heavy and may confuse “activity” with “authorization trust.”
  • Core idea: Build a directed endorsement graph from latest ERC-20 allowance edges (approve / permit → Approval logs), then apply weighted PageRank → EndorseRank.
  • Baseline: Adaptive Weighted PageRank (AWP) on time-decayed transfer graphs (Do et al.).
  • Validation style: Construct (domain) alignment — compare method ranks to observable wallet proxies with Spearman ρ and Kendall τ on the same wallets.

Scientific claim (current framing)

EndorseRank is a construct-distinct, computationally lighter social reputation score. It is not expected to dominate AWP on every proxy. The contribution is an efficiency–alignment trade-off with explicit parameters and a reproducible pipeline.

2 · What these parameters allow us to know

Interpretation guide (before numbers)

Sampling parameters (window, m, n)

  • Window + T_obs: fix which events exist and how old transfers are for AWP.
  • m = 3: success-rate denominators are not dominated by a single lucky/unlucky close.
  • Same n for both methods: τ differences are attributable to graph/method, not unequal samples.

Solver / graph parameters (d, τ_tol, k, t₀)

  • d = 0.85: how much score comes from network neighbors vs uniform teleport.
  • τ_tol, I_max: make runtime comparable; avoid “faster because stopped early.”
  • k, t₀: how strongly AWP prefers recent transfers — EndorseRank has no time decay (latest allowance wins).

Design principle

Pre-registration by configuration: we decide the experimental knobs first, then measure alignment and runtime. Robustness sweeps (damping, top tokens, sample size) test sensitivity; they do not redefine the primary setting after seeing τ.

3 · Preprocessing rules (what becomes an edge)

From raw logs to graph inputs

Approvals → latest allowances

  • Topic: Approval(address,address,uint256).
  • EIP-2612 permit ultimately emits the same Approval — no separate event type.
  • Per (owner, spender, token): keep chronologically last approval in the window.
  • Final zero allowance removes the edge.
  • Across tokens: sum positive latest amounts → weight w_A(u→v).
  • Unlimited approvals retained as large finite weights after decimals (strong authorization).

Transfers → time-decayed flows

  • Topic: Transfer(address,address,uint256).
  • Decimal-adjust value; keep timestamp, from, to, token.
  • Age Δt = days before T_obs.
  • Weight contribution: v_e · σ(Δt) with logistic σ.
  • Aggregate on directed edge u→v by summing contributions.
  • Addresses lower-cased for joins; self-transfers handled specially in Sybil proxies.

Subgraph restriction: retain edges with ≥1 endpoint in the cohort (neighbors can push mass into cohort scores). Evaluation reports scores only on W.

4 · Computation — EndorseRank

Allowance graph → weighted PageRank

Edge: owner u endorses spender v with weight w_A(u,v) = Σ_token a*(u,v,token)
Transition: P_{u,v} = w(u,v) / out-strength(u) if out-strength > 0
Iterate: r ← (1−d) π + d Pᵀ r + dangling redistribution · π   until ‖Δr‖₁ < 10⁻⁸ or 300 iters
Rank: dense rank of scores on cohort (1 = highest); address tie-break

Critical semantic choice

No temporal decay on allowances. A newer Approval for the same (owner, spender, token) overrides the older one. The graph is a snapshot of current spending permissions, not a history of all past approvals.

What the score means

High EndorseRank ≈ wallet sits in a position that receives multi-hop endorsement mass from allowance structure (who is authorized to spend by whom). It is not “this wallet made profitable trades.”

Matched-cohort graph size (benchmark table): 14,727 edges · PageRank wall-clock 2.105 s · peak mem ≈ 4.6 MB · ≈ 37 iterations.

4 · Computation — AWP

Transfer graph with logistic time decay

σ(Δt) = 1 / (1 + exp(k (Δt − t₀)))   with   k = 0.01,   t₀ = 180 days
w_T(u,v) = Σ_{transfers u→v} v_e · σ(Δt_e)
Same PageRank solver as EndorseRank (d = 0.85, τ_tol = 10⁻⁸, I_max = 300)
  • Recent transfers (Δt ≪ 180): σ ≈ 1 — nearly full value weight.
  • Older transfers (Δt ≫ 180): σ smoothly shrinks — history fades.
  • What the score means: high AWP ≈ wallet receives multi-hop, recency-weighted transfer mass (economic-flow centrality), not necessarily “authorized spender trust.”
  • Structural cost: denser graph → more work per iteration. Matched cohort: 137,087 edges · 18.711 s · ≈ 38.2 MB · ≈ 100 iterations.

H3 intuition: PageRank cost scales with |E|; sparse allowance graphs should recompute faster than dense transfer graphs under the same solver settings.

4 · Computation — GF-PR (supplementary only)

Outcome-native ceiling, not a social peer method

GF-PR builds a star graph from the same GMX PnL stream that defines trading-success proxies:

  • Profit close: edge POOL → wallet with weight x
  • Loss close: wallet → SINK with |x| + ε_loss (0.1)
  • Liquidation: wallet → SINK with |x| + ε_liq (1.0)
  • No wallet↔wallet social edges

What we learn from GF-PR

If ranking is allowed to read outcome flow directly, trading-success mean τ can reach 0.583 on this cohort. That is a construct-overlap ceiling, not evidence of better social reputation. Social methods should be judged far below this ceiling without being called “failures.”

4 · Proxies — five families

What we compare ranks against (and why)

Each proxy P is a wallet-level observable. Higher values are oriented toward “better / more central / safer / more successful” before correlation. For method M and proxy P we report Spearman ρ and Kendall τ between ranks on the same n wallets.

Transferin-degree; in-valueMethod order matches inbound economic activity (Do et al. baseline). Strong AWP↔transfer = within-construct coherence for transfer graphs.

Transfer & allowance proxies are local degree/value sums from the same event classes as the graphs — they check construct coherence. Inverse-risk / Sybil / trading-success are harder external tests.

4 · Proxies — operational definitions (selected)

How proxy numbers are calculated

Transfer / Allowance (local)

  • in_degree(w) = # distinct senders to w
  • in_value(w) = Σ inbound transfer values
  • in_approve_degree(w) = # owners with latest allowance to w as spender
  • in_approve_value(w) = Σ latest positive allowances to w

GMX-derived (external to social graphs)

  • loss_avoidance = −Σ min(x_c, 0) over closes
  • non_loss_close_rate = 1 − (ordinary loss closes / |C|)
  • Trading success: close-success counts, realized gains, success rate (min closes m=3)
  • Sybil family: unique counterparties / inbound txs; tenure days; active months

What Kendall τ tells us here

τ ≈ agreement of orderings (how often pairs of wallets are ranked the same way by method and proxy). We use bands: |τ| < 0.10 ≈ noise; 0.10–0.35 weak–moderate; ≥ 0.35 practically meaningful in comparable PageRank/blockchain ranking work. Values are compared relatively across methods on the same cohort — not as clinical cutoffs.

5 · Synthesis — what the whole parameter→measure chain shows

From knobs to knowledge claims

We can claim

  • H1: ER and AWP induce different rankings (inter-method τ = 0.316).
  • H2 (construct-specific): ER wins allowance; AWP wins transfer (and most outcome-adjacent social families).
  • H3: ER PageRank is substantially faster under matched solver settings.
  • RQ4: Transfer proxies are a stronger validation frame than trading-success for social methods here.

We should not claim

  • EndorseRank universally “beats” AWP on all proxies.
  • Social PageRank predicts GMX profitability well (τ small; GF-PR ceiling shows outcome overlap is a different construct).
  • Reputation can replace collateral — not supported; at best a monitoring / fee-tier signal hypothesis.

Operator reading: choose EndorseRank for authorization semantics + refresh latency; choose AWP for transfer-hub detection / outcome co-movement — with explicit τ tables, not a single headline score.

6 · Challenges encountered

Process difficulties (and how they changed the story)

Hypothesis reframing

Proposal-era hope of overall EndorseRank superiority failed on real data. Parameters were not retuned to force a win; the dissertation claim was rewritten as an efficiency–alignment trade-off with construct-specific τ.

Other issues

  • Aave wallet↔wallet baselines too sparse (τ ≈ 0.05–0.14) — archived, not primary.
  • Trading-success alignment weak for both social methods.
  • Pipeline hazards (e.g. fixtures overwriting real rankings) required restore discipline.
  • Registration working title vs current EndorseRank title still needs supervisor alignment.
