The financial system has generated broad-based growth impetus by allocating capital efficiently through lending. In this light, the advancement of DeFi is likewise expected to hinge critically on the degree of on-chain lending and leveraged trading activity. However, DeFi platforms face constraints in executing collateral-backed loans due to difficulties in enforcing collateral assets, and they similarly encounter limitations in offering unsecured credit, since traditional credit-scoring models that incorporate tangible-asset and employment information cannot be applied directly.

This dissertation proposes EndorseRank, an on-chain reputation metric leveraging ERC-20 approve and permit allowances as direct indicators of wallet endorsement. Empirical validation uses GMX V2 perpetual swap position outcomes on Arbitrum One.

\textbf{\emph{Keywords---}} DeFi, On-Chain Reputation, GMX V2, Perpetual Swap Positions, Arbitrum One, Blockchain Data Analytics, Wallet Endorsement Graph
