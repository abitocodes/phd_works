\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
\label{ch:abstract}

DeFi protocols require wallet reputation signals that are computationally scalable, semantically interpretable, and aligned with meaningful financial outcomes. This dissertation proposes \textbf{EndorseRank}, applying Weighted PageRank to ERC-20 allowance edges on Arbitrum One, and compares it against Adaptive Weighted PageRank (AWP) on transfer graphs.

On BigQuery-derived data ($n=5{,}521$ GMX-qualified wallets, six-month observation window), validation uses six proxy families: transfer (Do et al.\ baseline), allowance, GMX default/liquidation, inverse risk, Sybil-adjusted stability, and GMX trading success. EndorseRank runs $\approx 8\times$ faster than AWP and aligns more strongly with allowance proxies ($\tau=0.355$ vs.\ $-0.030$); AWP aligns more strongly with transfer and most outcome-based families. Inter-method rank correlation ($\tau=0.316$) confirms non-redundant reputation structures.

\textbf{\emph{Keywords---}} DeFi, EndorseRank, Adaptive Weighted PageRank, On-Chain Reputation, GMX V2, Arbitrum One, Domain Alignment, Spearman's rho, Kendall's tau
