\section{Evaluation Pipeline and Reproducibility}
\label{ch:appendix-eval}

This appendix documents the evaluation pipeline that produced the numerics in Chapter~\ref{ch:results}: data sources, processing stages, default parameters, proxy formulas, and a glossary. All quantities cited here also appear in the main-text tables and figures; no external files are required to interpret the claims.

\dissertationSubheading[sec:pipeline-stages]{Pipeline stages}

The evaluation proceeds in two stages on Arbitrum One over the observation window 2025-12-01 to 2026-05-31 (UTC, inclusive).

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Reputation graphs and scores:} Extract ERC-20 \texttt{Approval} and \texttt{Transfer} logs in monthly batches (per-query dry-run and a $150$\,GiB abort cap). The matched cohort is wallets with non-zero approval or transfer connectivity ($n=5{,}521$). Build the latest-allowance endorsement graph and the time-decayed transfer graph, then compute EndorseRank and AWP scores by power iteration (damping $d=0.85$, tolerance $10^{-8}$, at most $300$ iterations).
\item \textbf{Checks, alignment, and benchmarks:} Compute transfer and allowance checks defined in Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}, report Spearman $\rho$ and Kendall $\tau$ against each score vector, time PageRank solves on pre-built edges (three-run averages), and run robustness sweeps over damping, top tokens, and sample size.
\end{enumerate}

Offline synthetic checks use the same scoring and alignment logic on fixture graphs (no cloud access) to validate implementation before real extraction. Real extraction requires Google Cloud credentials and a billing project; scans target only the public Arbitrum One log dataset.

\dissertationSubheading[sec:bq-extraction]{BigQuery extraction design}

ERC-20 \texttt{Approval} and \texttt{Transfer} events are identified by their standard topic-$0$ hashes and restricted to logs whose owner/spender or from/to address lies in the evaluation wallet list, so scan cost scales with the cohort rather than the full chain. Monthly batching keeps each query under the operational byte cap. Representative SQL patterns appear in Appendix~\ref{ch:appendix-sql}.

Extraction records (bytes processed, row counts, timestamps) are retained in the study's acquisition log for auditability; this research reports the resulting cohort sizes and edge counts rather than those operational logs.

\dissertationSubheading[sec:delegation-deferred]{Credit-delegation extract (deferred)}

Aave V3 \texttt{BorrowAllowanceDelegated} events were not extracted in this study. Any exploratory ranking that depends on credit-delegation edges is therefore undefined on the present sample and is reported as non-interpretable ($\tau = \text{---}$) in the archived Aave-oriented matrix (Appendix~\ref{ch:appendix-archive}). Extending the study to delegation-based reputation would require a dedicated extract of those events and a fresh alignment evaluation; it is left to future work.

\dissertationSubheading[sec:proxy-appendix]{Proxy operational definitions (reference)}

The following formulas match Chapter~\ref{ch:methodology}.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Transfer:} $\mathrm{in\_degree}(w)=|\{u:\exists u\to w\}|$; $\mathrm{in\_value}(w)=\sum_{e:u\to w}\mathrm{value}(e)$.

\item \textbf{Allowance:} $\mathrm{in\_approve\_degree}(w)=|\{o:\exists o\to w\}|$; $\mathrm{in\_approve\_value}(w)=\sum_o w_a(o,w)$ with $w$ as spender.

\item \textbf{Sybil-adjusted stability:} $\mathrm{inbound\_counterparty\_ratio}(w)=|\{u:u\to w\}|/|T^{\mathrm{in}}(w)|$ (self-transfers excluded); tenure in days; active months.
\end{itemize}

\dissertationSubheading[sec:glossary]{Glossary}

\begin{description}
\item[Allowance] ERC-20 authorization for a spender to transfer tokens on behalf of an owner.
\item[Alignment] Rank agreement between reputation scores and a validation proxy ($\rho$, $\tau$).
\item[Construct overlap] Shared data source between a ranking method and a proxy, inflating alignment.
\item[Endorsement graph] Directed graph of latest allowances (EndorseRank input).
\item[Matched cohort] Primary sample of $n=5{,}521$ wallets with non-zero endorsement- or transfer-graph connectivity.
\item[Expanded wallet pool] Larger address set ($N=31{,}612$) for runtime scaling.
\item[PageRank] Spectral ranking by stationary distribution of a damped random walk.
\item[Proxy] Observable wallet metric used for domain-intuition validation.
\item[Teleport] Uniform jump in PageRank with probability $1-d$.
\end{description}
