\chapter*{Abstract}

\label{ch:abstract}

Decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols need on-chain wallet reputation scores that can be refreshed often, interpreted in operational terms, and checked against public ledger signals. Transfer-graph Adaptive Weighted PageRank (AWP) is a documented baseline for that task, but historical transfer aggregation is costly, and transfer volume is not the same as explicit spending authorization.

This research introduces EndorseRank: PageRank on a directed endorsement graph whose edges are the latest ERC-20 allowances on Arbitrum One. I evaluate EndorseRank against AWP on a matched wallet cohort ($n=5{,}521$) defined by GMX V2 activity ($\geq 3$ closes) solely as a sampling frame, not as a trading-skill test. Contemporaneous checks use transfer and allowance local statistics. A temporal holdout then scores wallets from events through 28~February~2026 and labels them from new owner--spender approvals in March--May~2026.

On the matched cohort, EndorseRank PageRank wall-clock time is $2.105$\,s versus $18.711$\,s for AWP ($\approx 8.9\times$). Allowance-family alignment is an intended-construct check, not an external test: EndorseRank mean Kendall $\tau=0.355$, while AWP is near zero ($-0.030$). AWP remains closer to transfer proxies ($\tau=0.534$ vs.\ $0.333$). Inter-method $\tau=0.316$ indicates overlapping but not interchangeable rankings.

On the holdout spender cohort ($n=1{,}335$), EndorseRank aligns with future new approvers at Kendall $\tau=0.479$ (bootstrap 95\% CI $0.426$--$0.529$); AWP is near zero ($\tau=0.044$). Two hundred twenty-two spenders received at least one new t2 approval pair. That result is the same endorsement construct in the future tense, not a prediction of trading profit, liquidation, or malicious spenders.

On an expanded wallet pool ($N=31{,}612$), EndorseRank remains $\approx 10.4\times$ faster than AWP at $n=10{,}000$ and $\approx 9.4\times$ at full pool size. Damping, top-20 token, and sample-size checks do not reverse the allowance--transfer trade-off. Protocols that need authorization-aware scores at low refresh cost have a rationale for EndorseRank; protocols that need transfer-hub detection may still prefer AWP.

\textbf{\emph{Keywords---}} DeFi, EndorseRank, Adaptive Weighted PageRank, On-Chain Reputation, ERC-20 allowance, temporal holdout, Arbitrum One, Kendall's tau
