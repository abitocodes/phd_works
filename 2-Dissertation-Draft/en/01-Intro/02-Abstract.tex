\chapter*{Abstract}

\label{ch:abstract}

Decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols need on-chain wallet reputation scores that can be refreshed often, interpreted in operational terms, and checked against public ledger signals. Transfer-graph Adaptive Weighted PageRank (AWP) is a documented baseline for that task, but historical transfer aggregation is costly, and transfer volume is not the same as explicit spending authorization.

This dissertation introduces EndorseRank: PageRank on a directed endorsement graph whose edges are the latest ERC-20 allowances on Arbitrum One. I evaluate EndorseRank against AWP on a matched wallet cohort ($n=5{,}521$) using five proxy families. I also incorporate GMX V2 trading-success proxies and add GF-PR as a supplementary outcome-native ceiling built from the same profit-and-loss stream; GF-PR is not a deployable social reputation score.

On the matched cohort, EndorseRank PageRank wall-clock time is $2.105$\,s versus $18.711$\,s for AWP ($\approx 8.9\times$). Allowance-family alignment is an intended-construct check, not an external test: EndorseRank mean Kendall $\tau=0.355$, while AWP is near zero ($-0.030$). AWP remains closer to transfer proxies ($\tau=0.534$ vs.\ $0.333$). Inter-method $\tau=0.316$ indicates overlapping but not interchangeable rankings.

On an expanded wallet pool ($N=31{,}612$), EndorseRank remains $\approx 10.4\times$ faster than AWP at $n=10{,}000$ and $\approx 9.4\times$ at full pool size. Damping, top-20 token, and sample-size checks do not reverse the allowance--transfer trade-off.

Among social methods, transfer proxies yield higher mean alignment than trading-success proxies on this chain. GF-PR reaches the highest trading-success mean $\tau$ ($0.583$) by construction. Protocols that need authorization-aware scores at low refresh cost have a rationale for EndorseRank; protocols that need transfer-hub detection may still prefer AWP.

\textbf{\emph{Keywords---}} DeFi, EndorseRank, Adaptive Weighted PageRank, On-Chain Reputation, GMX V2, Arbitrum One, Domain Alignment, Spearman's rho, Kendall's tau, GF-PR (outcome-native reference)
