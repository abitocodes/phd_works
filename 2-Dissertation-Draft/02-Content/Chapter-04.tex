\dissertationSubheading{Pipeline and dataset}

The evaluation pipeline (\texttt{A-Skill-Programs/margin\_rank/}) ingests (1) latest ERC-20 allowances, (2) time-stamped transfers, and (3) decoded GMX \texttt{PositionDecrease} events. EndorseRank and AWP scores are merged into \texttt{wallet\_rankings.parquet} via \texttt{compute\_reputation\_ranks.py}. Alignment uses six proxy families computed in \texttt{proxy\_metrics.py} and exported through \texttt{run\_dissertation\_eval.py --real --export-latex}.

Table~\ref{tab:dataset-stats} summarizes the Tier~1 BigQuery cohort ($n=5{,}521$ wallets with $\geq 3$ GMX closes and non-zero reputation subgraph connectivity).

\dissertationSubheading{Claim~2: Six-proxy alignment overview}

Table~\ref{tab:alignment-summary} reports mean Kendall $\tau$ between reputation scores and each proxy family. AWP aligns more strongly with transfer, Sybil-adjusted stability, inverse-risk, liquidation, and GMX-success proxies on this sample. EndorseRank achieves higher mean $\tau$ under the \emph{allowance} family---consistent with construct validity for allowance-based propagation---while remaining competitive on transfer proxies ($\tau=0.333$ vs.\ AWP $0.534$).

\dissertationSubheading{Non-transfer Aave W\textrightarrow W PageRank baselines}

Table~\ref{tab:alignment-six-aave-methods} compares EndorseRank and AWP with four PageRank variants built on Aave V3 \emph{wallet-to-wallet} edges distinct from ERC-20 \texttt{Transfer} and \texttt{Approval}: \textbf{LiqCall-PR} (liquidator$\rightarrow$user from \texttt{LiquidationCall}), \textbf{Borrow-PR} (initiator$\rightarrow$onBehalfOf when distinct), and \textbf{Repay-PR} (repayer$\rightarrow$user when distinct). Pool-leg borrow/repay diagnostics (pool$\rightarrow$borrower, repayer$\rightarrow$pool) are recorded in \texttt{eval\_summary.json} but excluded from the main matrix because they overlap LF-PR semantics.

Aave lending logs for the GMX cohort yield sparse W$\leftrightarrow$W subgraphs (LiqCall 45 edges, 0.9\% wallet coverage; Borrow-PR 484 edges, 9.1\%; Repay-PR 787 edges, 15.3\%). None of the Aave methods exceed AWP or EndorseRank on allowance proxies; Repay-PR achieves the highest non-transfer mean $\tau$ on transfer ($0.143$) and Sybil ($0.068$) among Aave variants but remains below AWP. \textbf{Delegation-PR} is omitted from alignment ($\tau$ undefined) because Credit Delegation BQ extraction is deferred---scores are degenerate on this run.

\dissertationSubheading{Rank divergence (H1)}

EndorseRank and AWP produce non-redundant orderings: inter-method Kendall $\tau$ between the two score vectors is $0.316$ on the matched cohort ($n=5{,}521$), confirming distinct reputation structures (RQ1).

\dissertationSubheading{Summary of empirical claims}

\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textbf{C1.}] EndorseRank runtime $<$ AWP runtime on identical $n$ (Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-runtime}).
\item[\textbf{C2.}] Both methods achieve credible Spearman $\rho$/Kendall $\tau$ on selected proxies under Do et al.'s framework; family-level alignment varies by construct (Tables~\ref{tab:alignment-transfer}--\ref{tab:alignment-gmx}).
\item[\textbf{C3.}] \textbf{Construct-specific alignment:} EndorseRank exceeds AWP on allowance proxies; AWP exceeds EndorseRank on transfer and most outcome-based families (Table~\ref{tab:alignment-summary}).
\item[\textbf{C4.}] \textbf{Efficiency--alignment trade-off:} EndorseRank offers $\approx 8\times$ lower runtime and a sparser graph while maintaining moderate transfer alignment ($\tau \approx 0.33$) and stronger allowance alignment ($\tau \approx 0.35$).
\end{enumerate}
