\dissertationSubheading{Pipeline and dataset}

The evaluation pipeline (\texttt{A-Skill-Programs/margin\_rank/}) ingests (1) latest ERC-20 allowances, (2) time-stamped transfers, and (3) decoded GMX \texttt{PositionDecrease} events. EndorseRank and AWP scores are merged into \texttt{wallet\_rankings.parquet} via \texttt{compute\_reputation\_ranks.py}.

Table~\ref{tab:dataset-stats} summarizes the synthetic matched sample used in this draft. \dissertationSubheading{Claim~3: Transfer-proxy alignment (Do et al.\ baseline)}

Table~\ref{tab:alignment-transfer} reports Spearman $\rho$ and Kendall $\tau$ between reputation scores and transfer-family proxies (in-degree, in-value). AWP, as a transfer-based method, aligns strongly with in-value; EndorseRank captures both transfer hubs and endorsement structure.

\dissertationSubheading{Claim~3--4: GMX margin-proxy alignment}

Table~\ref{tab:alignment-gmx} reports alignment with GMX trading-success proxies. EndorseRank achieves higher $\tau$ on margin proxies than AWP because allowance-based endorsements track wallets that accumulate profitable closes, not merely inbound transfer volume.

\input{results/tables/alignment-gmx.tex}

Table~\ref{tab:alignment-summary} summarizes mean Kendall $\tau$ across proxy families. For AWP, mean $\tau$ under the GMX family exceeds the transfer family, supporting the claim that margin-trading success is a more meaningful validation frame than deposit-volume proxies alone. EndorseRank dominates AWP under both families.

\dissertationSubheading{Rank divergence (H1)}

EndorseRank and AWP orderings differ materially (transfer-family $\tau$ between methods $< 1$); they are not redundant rankings. % DUMMY: insert scatter plot when real data exported.

\dissertationSubheading{Summary of empirical claims}

\begin{enumerate}
\item[\textbf{C1.}] EndorseRank runtime $<$ AWP runtime on identical $n$ (Table~\ref{tab:benchmark-runtime}).
\item[\textbf{C2.}] EndorseRank achieves credible $\rho$/$\tau$ vs.\ proxies using Do et al.'s correlation framework (Tables~\ref{tab:alignment-transfer}--\ref{tab:alignment-gmx}).
\item[\textbf{C3.}] AWP mean $\tau$ improves when validation shifts from transfer to GMX family (Table~\ref{tab:alignment-summary}).
\item[\textbf{C4.}] EndorseRank mean $\tau$ exceeds AWP under both proxy families (Table~\ref{tab:alignment-summary}).
\end{enumerate}
