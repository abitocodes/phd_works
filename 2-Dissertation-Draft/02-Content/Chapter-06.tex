\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\dissertationSubheading{Summary of findings}

This dissertation formulated EndorseRank, an allowance-based Weighted PageRank reputation metric on Arbitrum One, and compared it against an AWP reproduction on an identical Tier~1 wallet sample ($n=5{,}521$) validated across six proxy families.

\begin{itemize}
\item EndorseRank required less wall-clock time and operated on a sparser graph than AWP (Claim~1; $\approx 2.9$\,s vs.\ $\approx 24$\,s).
\item Spearman $\rho$ and Kendall $\tau$ demonstrated construct-dependent alignment: EndorseRank on allowance proxies ($\tau \approx 0.35$), AWP on transfer proxies ($\tau \approx 0.53$) and several outcome families (Claim~2--3).
\item Neither method universally dominates; results support an efficiency--alignment trade-off (Claim~4).
\item Inter-method $\tau = 0.316$ confirms distinct reputation orderings (RQ1).
\end{itemize}

\dissertationSubheading{Original contribution to knowledge}

The study contributes allowance-based endorsement graph modeling, a six-proxy validation methodology for leveraged DeFi, and a reproducible open-source evaluation pipeline linking LaTeX results to \texttt{eval\_summary.json}.

\dissertationSubheading{Future work}

\begin{itemize}
\item Aave or cross-chain lending default labels where volume permits stronger credit-ground-truth validation.
\item Staged computational benchmarks (10k / 50k / 100k wallets) from cached parquet subgraphs.
\item Cross-protocol validation (GMX cross-chain, other L2s).
\item Adversarial manipulation detection and Sybil clustering on $G_{\text{approve}}$.
\item Integration with live risk oracles and governance tooling.
\end{itemize}
