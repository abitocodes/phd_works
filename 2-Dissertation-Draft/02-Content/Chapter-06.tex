\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\dissertationSubheading{Summary of findings}

This dissertation formulated EndorseRank, an allowance-based Weighted PageRank reputation metric on Arbitrum One, and compared it against an AWP reproduction on an identical wallet sample validated against GMX V2 trading-success proxies.

On synthetic offline data (to be replaced with BigQuery extraction): % DUMMY
\begin{itemize}
\item EndorseRank required less wall-clock time and operated on a sparser graph than AWP (Claim~1).
\item Spearman $\rho$ and Kendall $\tau$ demonstrated credible domain alignment under Do et al.'s correlation framework (Claim~2).
\item AWP alignment improved when validation shifted from transfer in-degree/in-value to GMX margin proxies (Claim~3).
\item EndorseRank outperformed AWP on mean $\tau$ under both proxy families (Claim~4).
\end{itemize}

\dissertationSubheading{Original contribution to knowledge}

The study contributes allowance-based endorsement graph modeling for on-chain reputation, a dual-proxy validation methodology for leveraged DeFi, and a reproducible open-source evaluation pipeline linking LaTeX results to \texttt{eval\_summary.json}.
