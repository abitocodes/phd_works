\chapter{Research Methodology}
\label{ch:methodology}

\dissertationSubheading{Data sources and sampling}

\textbf{Primary chain:} Arbitrum One (chain ID 42161).

\textbf{Observation window:} 2025-12-01 00:00 UTC through 2026-05-31 23:59:59 UTC (six months).

\textbf{BigQuery source:} \texttt{bigquery-public-data.goog\_blockchain\_arbitrum\_one\_us.logs} (SQL templates in \texttt{A-Skill-Programs/margin\_rank/sql/}; acquisition plan in \texttt{docs/bigquery\_data\_plan.md}).

\textbf{Reputation events:} ERC-20 \texttt{Approval} logs (EndorseRank) and \texttt{Transfer} logs (AWP), extracted in six monthly batches for the Phase~1 wallet cohort.

\textbf{GMX events:} \texttt{PositionDecrease} from GMX V2 \texttt{EventEmitter} (\texttt{0xC8ee91A54287DB53897056e12D9819156D3822Fb}) over the full observation window, identifying traders via the event \texttt{account} field.

\dissertationSubheading{Three-phase collection strategy}

\textbf{Phase 1 (Stream A):} Extract and decode all GMX \texttt{PositionDecrease} logs; retain wallets with $\geq 3$ closes (Tier~1 alignment cohort; $n=5{,}521$ after BigQuery extraction).

\textbf{Phase 2 (Streams B \& C):} Wallet-filtered ERC-20 \texttt{Approval} and \texttt{Transfer} events for the Tier~1 cohort; preprocess to latest allowances and time-decayed transfer edges.

\textbf{Phase 3 (local):} Six-proxy alignment evaluation and computational benchmarks on the full Tier~1 cohort from cached parquet---no additional BigQuery scans.

\textbf{Sample:} Intersection of wallets with $\geq 3$ GMX closes and non-zero reputation subgraph connectivity ($n=5{,}521$).

\textbf{Cost guardrails:} Mandatory \texttt{--dry-run} before extraction; 150 GiB per-query cap. Bytes logged in \texttt{data/processed/extraction\_manifest.json}.

\dissertationSubheading{Graph construction}

\textbf{EndorseRank graph} $G_{\text{approve}}$: nodes are wallets; directed edges owner $\to$ spender weighted by latest decimal-adjusted allowance sum per pair; PageRank damping $d=0.85$.

\textbf{AWP graph} $G_{\text{transfer}}$: directed transfer edges with logistic time-decay $\sigma(\Delta t) = 1/(1+\exp(k(\Delta t - t_0)))$ on edge weights (Do et al., 2023); $k=0.01$, $t_0=180$ days, observation end 2026-05-31 UTC.

Both methods operate on the \emph{same} wallet seed set; differences in results are attributable to ranking logic, not sampling inconsistency.

\dissertationSubheading{Six-proxy evaluation design}

For each method $M \in \{\text{EndorseRank}, \text{AWP}\}$ and proxy $P$, compute reputation scores $\mathbf{s}_M$, proxy values $\mathbf{p}_P$, and report Spearman's $\rho$ and Kendall's $\tau$ between rankings. All proxies are oriented so higher values imply higher expected reputation before correlation. Six families are evaluated (definitions in \texttt{margin\_config.yaml} and \texttt{proxy\_metrics.py}):

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Transfer} (Do et al.\ baseline): in-degree, in-value.
\item \textbf{Allowance}: in-approve degree, in-approve value (wallet as spender).
\item \textbf{Default/liquidation} (GMX): liquidation-free rate, zero-liquidation flag, liquidation-free closes.
\item \textbf{Inverse risk} (GMX PnL): loss avoidance, non-loss close rate, worst-close PnL score.
\item \textbf{Sybil-adjusted stability}: inbound counterparty ratio, transfer tenure, active months.
\item \textbf{GMX success}: close-success count, realized gain proxy, close success rate.
\end{enumerate}

Family-level mean $\tau$ summarizes construct alignment; \texttt{method\_comparison} in \texttt{eval\_summary.json} records which method aligns more strongly per family. Lending-protocol default labels are excluded due to insufficient Arbitrum volume within scope.

\dissertationSubheading{Computational benchmark (Claim~1)}

On identical inputs, measure over five runs: wall-clock runtime (graph build + PageRank), peak memory (tracemalloc), PageRank iterations to convergence, and edge counts. Implementation: \texttt{scripts/benchmark\_runtime.py} (invoked from \texttt{run\_dissertation\_eval.py}).

\dissertationSubheading{Reproducibility}

\texttt{scripts/run\_dissertation\_eval.py --real} recomputes proxies, alignment, and benchmarks from cached parquet. \texttt{--fixtures} remains available for offline synthetic runs. Outputs: \texttt{data/processed/eval\_summary.json} and LaTeX fragments under \texttt{2-Dissertation-Draft/results/tables/}.

\dissertationSubheading{Ethics}

Only public on-chain data are used; no off-chain identity linkage. See approved proposal \texttt{1-Proposal/proposal/02-Content/Chapter-07.tex} for ethics clearance details.
