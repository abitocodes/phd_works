\chapter{Research Methodology}
\label{ch:methodology}

\dissertationSubheading{Data sources and sampling}

\textbf{Primary chain:} Arbitrum One (chain ID 42161).

\textbf{Observation window:} 2025-12-01 00:00 UTC through 2026-05-31 23:59:59 UTC (six months).

\textbf{BigQuery source:} \texttt{bigquery-public-data.goog\_blockchain\_arbitrum\_one\_us.logs} (SQL templates in \texttt{A-Skill-Programs/margin\_rank/sql/}; acquisition plan in \texttt{docs/bigquery\_data\_plan.md}).

\textbf{Reputation events:} ERC-20 \texttt{Approval} logs (EndorseRank) and \texttt{Transfer} logs (AWP), extracted in six monthly batches for the Phase~1 wallet cohort.

\textbf{GMX events:} \texttt{PositionDecrease} from GMX V2 \texttt{EventEmitter} (\texttt{0xC8ee91A54287DB53897056e12D9819156D3822Fb}) over the full observation window, identifying traders via the event \texttt{account} field.

\dissertationSubheading{Three-phase collection strategy}

\textbf{Phase 1 (Stream A):} Extract and decode all GMX \texttt{PositionDecrease} logs; retain wallets with $\geq 3$ closes (Tier~1 alignment cohort; expected $n \approx 500$--3,000, confirmed after extraction).

\textbf{Phase 2 (Streams B \& C):} Wallet-filtered ERC-20 \texttt{Approval} and \texttt{Transfer} events for the Tier~1 cohort (plus benchmark seed addresses); preprocess to latest allowances and time-decayed transfer edges.

\textbf{Phase 3 (local):} Alignment evaluation and staged computational benchmarks (10k $\to$ 50k $\to$ 100k wallets) by subsampling cached parquet subgraphs---no additional BigQuery scans for benchmark scaling.

\textbf{Sample (current draft):} Intersection of wallets with $\geq 3$ GMX closes and non-zero reputation subgraph connectivity. % DUMMY: $n=571$ synthetic placeholder; replace with Tier~1 BigQuery extraction before final submission.

\textbf{Cost guardrails:} Mandatory \texttt{--dry-run} before extraction; 150 GiB per-query cap; target $\leq$1 TiB/month (BigQuery free tier, \$0). Bytes logged in \texttt{data/processed/extraction\_manifest.json}.

\dissertationSubheading{Graph construction}

\textbf{EndorseRank graph} $G_{\text{approve}}$: nodes are wallets; directed edges owner $\to$ spender weighted by latest decimal-adjusted allowance sum per pair; PageRank damping $d=0.85$.

\textbf{AWP graph} $G_{\text{transfer}}$: directed transfer edges with logistic time-decay $\sigma(\Delta t) = 1/(1+\exp(k(\Delta t - t_0)))$ on edge weights (Do et al., 2023); $k=0.01$, $t_0=180$ days, observation end 2026-05-31 UTC.

Both methods operate on the \emph{same} wallet seed set; differences in results are attributable to ranking logic, not sampling inconsistency.

\dissertationSubheading{Dual-proxy evaluation design}

For each method $M \in \{\text{EndorseRank}, \text{AWP}\}$ and proxy $P$:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Compute reputation score vector $\mathbf{s}_M$ and proxy value vector $\mathbf{p}_P$ over matched wallets.
\item Rank wallets by $\mathbf{s}_M$ and by $\mathbf{p}_P$ separately.
\item Report Spearman's $\rho$ and Kendall's $\tau$ between the two rankings.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Claim~3 test:} Compare AWP mean Kendall $\tau$ under transfer family vs.\ GMX family. Improvement ($\tau_{\text{GMX}} > \tau_{\text{transfer}}$) indicates that margin-success validation is more semantically aligned than deposit-volume validation for this domain.

\textbf{Claim~4 test:} EndorseRank must exceed AWP on mean $\tau$ within \emph{both} families.

\dissertationSubheading{Computational benchmark (Claim~1)}

On identical inputs, measure over five runs: wall-clock runtime (graph build + PageRank), peak memory (tracemalloc), PageRank iterations to convergence, and edge counts. Benchmark wallet counts expand in stages (10k, 50k, 100k) from locally cached graphs after Tier~1 extraction. Implementation: \texttt{scripts/benchmark\_runtime.py}.
